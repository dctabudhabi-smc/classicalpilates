About me

From the dance world to the Pilates method.

Coming from the dance world and having a back injury, I first discovered Pilates in Athens back in 2006, after I finished my MA in Performance Studies in the U.K.

I finished my Pilates training in 2008, taught by Evgenia Papadopoulou, a student of Romana Kryzanowska. My passion for always going deeper into the method has grown ever since.

“Pilates for me is the perfect source of life. Working hard to find your inner strength.”

The Pilates Method

“Contrology is not a system of haphazard exercises designed to produce only bulging muscles.”

Joseph Pilates, 1883-1967

Joseph Hubertus Pilates was the mastermind who created Contrology; what we call today the Pilates method.

The Pilates method is suitable for all ages, backgrounds and fitness levels. It develops your strength, flexibility, mobility and endurance, and keeps your body and mind alert and motivated.

Pilates E-Motion, Classical Educational School.

E-motion is energy in motion.

Founded and directed by Gloria Gasperi in 2013, Pilates E-Motion is dedicated to preserving the essence of Classical Pilates while inspiring students and teachers worldwide. Gloria trained with Jay Grimes, one of the last direct students of Joe and Clara Pilates.

Roi Karpathiou is the School’s representative in Greece.

Teacher Training Program

Become a classical Pilates teacher with Pilates E-Motion.

Two sections, three modules each, and a final exam. Offered over weekends or as intensive weeks.

In the weekend format, each Mat + Reformer module runs over 3 weekends and each Cadillac + Barrels + Chairs module over 2 weekends.In the intensive format, each module is one intensive week of training. Each section ends with a final exam.
