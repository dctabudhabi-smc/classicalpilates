Teacher Training Program

In collaboration between Roi Karpathiou and Gloria Gasperi.

Pilates E-Motion in Greece

Learn the classical method, find your own voice.

Pilates E-Motion is a Classical Pilates School founded and directed by Gloria Gasperi. Roi Karpathiou is the School’s local representative in Greece.

With Gloria’s support and participation, Roi shares the Teacher Training Program with students in Greece and is your first point of contact for questions and for arranging your admission evaluation.

Program structure

Two sections. Three modules each.

The Program is offered in two formats: over weekends, or as intensive weeks. Choose a format to see how the modules run.

In the weekend format, each Mat + Reformer module runs over 3 weekends and each Cadillac + Barrels + Chairs module over 2 weekends.In the intensive format, each module is one intensive week of training. Each section ends with a final exam.

The program

An approach that leads to a clear result.

01

Program goals

The goal of the program is to give students the tools to understand the Method, feel the exercises in their own bodies, and learn to read the bodies in front of them, in order to work on the specific needs of each person they teach.

02

Teaching approach

The School is based on a specific teaching approach that leads to certain results: giving each student the possibility to find their own unique voice, in respect of the Method and the original intention of Joe’s work.

03

Anatomy and biomechanics

Anatomy and biomechanics are approached in a unique way, deeply connected to the work itself, in order to understand why the work is taught in a certain way, in respect of the natural functioning of the human body.

04

A supportive network

The School has a strong, connected network of teachers. Students are followed and supported in their path by Gloria herself, her team of Teachers’ Trainers, and the wider network of all the School’s Teachers, in a collaborative, serene, supportive and healthy environment.

05

A personalised path

The path is progressive and as personalised as possible, following the specific needs, uniqueness and time progression of every student.

Presence, attention, ability to feel and see, sense of responsibility, awareness, motivation, acceptance, intention, instinct, intuition and passion.

Some of the qualities and skills the School works to cultivate and renew in every student
