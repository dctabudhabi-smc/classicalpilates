About the Pilates Method

Contrology.

Joseph Hubertus Pilates was the mastermind who created Contrology; what we call today the Pilates method.

Joseph Pilates, 1883-1967

“Contrology develops the body uniformly, corrects wrong postures, restores physical vitality, invigorates the mind and elevates the spirit.”

For everybody

Strength, flexibility, mobility and endurance.

The Pilates method is suitable for all ages, backgrounds and fitness levels.

It develops your strength, flexibility, mobility and endurance, and keeps your body and mind alert and motivated. In my studio the method is taught as Joseph Pilates taught it: the full system, on the mat and on the original apparatus.
