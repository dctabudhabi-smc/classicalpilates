The School

Movement that connects body, mind and spirit.

At Pilates E-Motion, movement goes beyond exercise. It becomes a way to connect body, mind, and spirit.

Founded and directed by Gloria Gasperi, the School is dedicated to preserving the essence of Classical Pilates while inspiring students and teachers worldwide.

Its goal is to educate people to feel and understand the work in their own bodies, so they can then use this awareness to truly understand the needs of their clients. Pilates is a mind, body and spirit practice, rooted in Joe’s original vision: everything is connected, our body, our mind, our emotions.

Founder & Director

Gloria Gasperi

An Italian teacher and founder of Pilates E-Motion School. “For me, Pilates is not only movement; it’s a way to return to life, to reconnect body, mind, and emotions.”

01

My story begins

Movement has been part of Gloria since childhood. Sports opened the door to a deeper way of living and expressing herself through the body. At 17 she began teaching movement and soon after discovered Pilates, an encounter that forever changed her path.

02

From Italy to the world

Curious and determined, she travelled to Holland and then the USA to deepen her studies. In Boston she trained with Clare Dunphy. Later, in Los Angeles, she met her teacher and mentor Jay Grimes, one of the last direct students of Joe and Clara Pilates, and found her true voice as a teacher at Vintage Pilates.

03

Returning to Italy

Back home, with the desire to spread Pilates in its truest, most authentic form, she founded Pilates E-Motion School in 2013: a place where tradition, science and passion come together, and where students can grow into confident, authentic teachers.

04

Mission and values

To honour Joe Pilates’ original vision: movement inspired by nature, animals and children, helping us “return to life”, so that every student discovers their truest self through the work.
